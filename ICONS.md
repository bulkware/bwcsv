# bwCSV icons

This document identifies the source and licensing of the icon assets bundled with bwCSV.

## Application icons

The bwCSV application icon and project-created artwork are licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Source: <https://github.com/bulkware/bwcsv>

Applies to:

- `src/bwcsv/assets/icon.png`
- `data/icons/bwcsv.ico`
- `data/icons/hicolor/512x512/apps/org.bulkware.bwcsv.png`

## Oxygen icons

The toolbar icons come from the KDE Oxygen icon theme and are licensed under LGPL-3.0-or-later.

Source: <https://develop.kde.org/frameworks/oxygen-icons/>

Applies to: `src/bwcsv/assets/about.png`, `fseparator.png`, `hheader.png`, `open_file.png`,
`quit.png`, `setheader.png`, `tdelimiter.png`, and `vheader.png`.
